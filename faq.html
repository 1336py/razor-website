Buying Products Support Policies

Buying

What is Razor?
Razor is a premium software platform focused on reliable, optimized solutions with a strong focus on performance, security and customer support. Our products get continuous improvements and updates to give you the best possible experience.
How do I purchase a product?
Open a product in the shop, choose a plan and select Buy now. Checkout gives you an order ID and our Litecoin address. If you have questions before buying, our support team is available in Discord.
What payment methods do you accept?
Only crypto (Litecoin) is accepted on the site. We accept many other methods in our Discord server, so open a ticket to ask.
How do discount codes work?
Enter your code on the product page. A valid code is saved to your order details, and staff apply the discount when they verify your order in Discord.
How do I receive my product after purchase?
After your payment confirms, open a ticket in our Discord and send your order ID and transaction ID. Once the payment is verified, our team delivers your product.

Products

Are Razor products compatible with Windows 10 and Windows 11?
Compatibility depends on the individual product. Each product is built to support as many systems as possible. Ask in Discord if you want to confirm before buying.
Do I receive updates after purchasing?
Yes, according to the plan you bought. We continuously work on reliability, compatibility and the overall experience.
Can I try a product before purchasing?
Trials aren't available due to the nature of our products. The shorter plans are the cheapest way to start.
Can I transfer my purchase to another account or device?
No. Transferring a purchase between devices is prohibited.

Support

Do you provide customer support?
Yes. Razor provides support through our official Discord server: purchase questions, product information, troubleshooting and general requests.
How long does support take to respond?
It depends on current volume and availability. We aim to respond as quickly as possible, normally within an hour of a ticket being opened.
Where can I get support?
Our official support channel is Discord. It's the fastest and most reliable way to reach the Razor team about products, purchases and help.

Policies

Are purchases refundable?
Refund eligibility depends on the circumstances and whether the product has already been delivered or used. See our Terms of Service for full details.
Is my information secure?
Razor takes customer privacy and security seriously. We only collect the information needed to provide our services and keep the experience reliable.
